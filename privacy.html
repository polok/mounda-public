Privacy policy

Mounda app. Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: your photos, measurements, jobs, company details and logo stay on your phone. There is no account and no server. The app sends only anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, never photos, notes, names, dimensions, quantities or prices. No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold.

What Mounda does

Mounda estimates how much loose material is in a pile, and how much to buy for a layer, from a photo you take or choose, or from dimensions you type.

Your photos

Photos are used on your phone to draw lines and work out the estimate. They are not uploaded and not saved. Only the result is kept: material, volume, weight, truckloads and date.

Data on your device

Saved measurements, jobs, your company details and logo, and the client details you type in (name, phone, address, notes) stay on your phone. There is no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes them. A PDF is created only when you share it, and goes only where you send it.

Camera and photo library

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo. For your library, Mounda receives only the photos you pick.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Mounda never sees your payment details or Apple ID, only whether a subscription is active.

Usage statistics and crash reports

Mounda uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. They receive anonymous events (for example that a measurement was saved or that the paywall was shown), the app version, the phone model and iOS version, a random installation identifier and, if the app crashes, a crash report. They never receive your photos, notes, dimensions, quantities, prices, material names or your Apple ID. This is used only to fix problems and improve the app. Mounda has no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites. Google processes the data under its own privacy policy.

Feedback form

The support page links to a Google Form where you can report an idea or a problem. What you type there reaches us through Google Forms and is covered by Google's privacy policy. The app does not use the form, and using it is optional.

Changes

If what the app collects changes, we will update this page and say so in the app.
