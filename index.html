How much is in the pile, and how much to buy

Photograph a pile of sand, gravel, crushed stone or soil, mark its width and height, and get the volume, the weight and the number of truckloads. Plan a layer, keep every delivery under a job, and send a PDF offer with your logo, transport and VAT.

Measure from a photo

Put a sheet of paper, a pallet or a brick next to the pile for scale, then mark its width and height.

How much to buy

Area and thickness, with compaction and spare, give the quantity, the weight, the trips and the cost.

Your materials

Sand, gravel, crushed stone, soil, rubble, mulch and firewood, plus ready-made local materials. Change any density, compaction or price.

Jobs and trips

Keep each delivery under a client or a project, repeat a trip with one tap, and see totals for any period.

PDFs and offers

Share a one-page PDF, one per job, or a report of what went to whom. Companies add name, tax ID, logo, transport and VAT.

Your truck and units

Set your own truck in tonnes or cubic metres. Metric or US units. Eight languages in the app.

Private

No account and no ads. Photos are not stored; your data stays on your phone.

Every result is an estimate, usually within 20–30%. Free for the first 14 days; after that, measuring from a photo needs a Mounda Pro subscription (monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial). Calculators, jobs, history and PDFs stay free.
