Frequently asked questions

How do I measure a pile from a photo?

Tap Photo and take or choose a picture. Put something of known size next to the pile (an A4 sheet, a pallet or a brick) at the same distance. Drag the yellow line over it, then the orange line across the pile's width and the blue line along its height. Pick the material and the shape and read the volume, weight and truckloads.

How accurate is it?

It is an estimate, usually within 20–30%. The photo is scaled flat, so the reference and the pile must be at about the same distance, and the real weight depends on moisture and grading. You can change the density of any material in More → Materials and prices.

How much do I need to buy for a layer?

Tap Layer, enter the area (or length and width, or measure it from a photo) and the thickness. Compaction and spare are included; add a price per tonne to see the cost.

What is free, and what needs a subscription?

Everything is free for the first 14 days. After that, measuring from a photo needs Mounda Pro (monthly or yearly, with a 7-day free trial). The calculators, jobs, history and PDFs stay free.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?

Open the paywall (More → Buy access) and tap Restore. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Mounda Pro.

Can I put my company on the PDFs?

Yes. Open More → Profile and choose Company. Add your name, tax ID, contact details and logo, your truck, a transport price per trip and VAT. They are printed on every PDF, and the offer adds transport and VAT to the cost of the material.

Can I use US units?

Yes. In More → Profile, choose US (ft, ton). The app follows your region by default.

Where are my materials from?

On the first launch Mounda adds ready-made materials for your region (for example Polish, American, German, Italian, Spanish, Chinese or Japanese products). You can edit or delete them, add more from More → Materials and prices, or add your own.

Where is my data, and what if I change phones?

Only on your phone, with no account. It moves with an iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes the data.
